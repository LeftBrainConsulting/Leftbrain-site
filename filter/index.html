How The Filter Works
Everything in one ranked list. Here's how it works.

Every improvement idea — AI projects, system upgrades, process changes — gets submitted to a single intake and scored by whoever submitted it, on the same three criteria every time — Impact 40, Urgency 40, Audience 20. The ranked list starts the conversation. Leadership finishes it in one meeting. What activates is what the team can actually absorb.

When every initiative is visible and ranked, your capital flows to the work with the highest return — and that's how EBITDA compounds. Once it's running, the ranked list quietly shows you the next capability worth building, exactly when you're ready for it.
